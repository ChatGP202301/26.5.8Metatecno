Electrolyzer cell manufacturing and retrofit

Ion Membrane Electrolyzer Cell Manufacturing and Retrofit

Metatecno manufactures electrolyzer cells and provides repair, coating and membrane-gap retrofit services for chlor-alkali ion membrane electrolyzers. The work is reviewed according to drawings, cell structure, coating condition, sealing surface condition and operating requirements.

Contact Engineer Send Drawing

Service scope

The electrolyzer cell business covers new cell manufacturing, maintenance of existing ion membrane electrolyzers, membrane-gap retrofit, frame palladium plating and titanium-palladium alloy retrofit. The same engineering review also supports metal-anode electrolyzer cells and chlorate electrolyzer manufacturing or repair.

Electrolyzer cell manufacturing and retrofit

Technical capabilities

The service can be reviewed for DD350, UHDE-type, INEOS-type, Asahi Kasei-type, Bluestar, Chlorine Engineers and other ion membrane electrolyzer platforms. Model names are used only to describe compatibility and maintenance application.

Cell manufacturing

Manufacturing is arranged according to confirmed drawings, cell configuration, material requirements and inspection requirements.

Repair and coating

Used cells can be evaluated for sealing surface repair, coating condition, frame condition and dimensional restoration.

Membrane-gap retrofit

Existing equipment can be reviewed for membrane-gap conversion to support higher current density operation after engineering confirmation.

Frame retrofit

Frame palladium plating and titanium-palladium alloy conversion are handled according to the operating medium and cell structure.

Chlorate cell service

Manufacturing and repair support is available for chlorate electrolyzer cells where drawings and operating conditions are provided.

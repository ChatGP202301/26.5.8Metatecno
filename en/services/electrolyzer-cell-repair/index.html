Electrolyzer maintenance service

Electrolyzer Cell Repair and Membrane Gap Retrofit

Metatecno supports chlor-alkali plants with electrolyzer cell inspection, repair, sealing surface restoration and membrane gap retrofit. Work scope is confirmed by photos, drawings, service history, medium and operating conditions before quotation.

Three repair approaches

The repair route is selected after incoming inspection. Cell structure, mesh condition, corrosion, sealing surfaces and the project objective determine whether the mesh remains assembled or is removed.

A / ASSEMBLED

Whole-electrode recoating

For suitable monopolar electrodes with serviceable mesh, the frame and backplate are repaired as required before the electrode assembly is prepared and recoated.

B / DISASSEMBLED

Mesh removal and recoating

For bipolar cells, damaged mesh or corrosion, the mesh is removed for separate repair or coating before controlled reassembly.

C / RETROFIT

Anode coating and cathode retrofit

For suitable structures, the anode can be recoated while the cathode receives reviewed elastic elements and face mesh for a membrane-gap retrofit.

DD350, UHDE, INEOS, Asahi Kasei, Bluestar and Chlorine Engineers names are used only to identify compatibility requirements. They do not imply OEM affiliation, endorsement or authorization.

Nine-step repair workflow

Each step is confirmed against the approved repair scope. Photos show representative workshop operations, not guaranteed results or a universal specification.

  1. 01. Incoming inspectionRecord cell condition, damage and required work.
  2. 02. DisassemblySeparate mesh or components when the approved route requires it.
  3. 03. Repair and grindingRestore approved frame, plate and sealing-surface areas.
  4. 04. Cleaning and leak checkClean, inspect and check the repaired structure.
  5. 05. Surface preparationPrepare anode and cathode mesh for the confirmed coating route.
  6. 06. CoatingApply and process the coating within the agreed work scope.
  7. 07. InspectionCarry out the specified coating and component checks.
  8. 08. Reassembly or retrofitWeld the approved mesh and complete the confirmed cathode work.
  9. 09. Packing and releaseComplete final review, protection and shipment preparation.

Service scope

Service scope

This service is intended for ion membrane electrolyzer maintenance projects where used cells require evaluation before reuse, repair or retrofit.

Cell inspection

We review visible damage, sealing surfaces, frame condition and connection areas before proposing the repair method.

Sealing surface restoration

The work focuses on gasket seats, frame contact surfaces and areas that influence sealing compression.

Membrane gap retrofit

For suitable cells, membrane gap retrofit is reviewed according to structure, plate condition and operation target.

Coating and frame work

Coating repair, frame palladium plating and titanium-palladium frame retrofit are evaluated project by project.

Applicable platforms

Applicable to DD350, UHDE, INEOS, Asahi Kasei, Bluestar, Chlorine Engineers and other platforms after verification.

Quotation basis

Quotation requires photos, drawings, quantity, platform, damage position, medium, temperature, pressure and service history.

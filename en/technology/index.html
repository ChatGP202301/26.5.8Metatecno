Fluoroplastic Compression Molding

Fluoroplastic compression molding is used to process sheets, rods, sleeves, tapes, sealing rings, diaphragms, and parts with metal inserts. The process consists of three main steps: preforming, sintering, and cooling.

Process Steps

1. Preforming

PTFE powder is uniformly added to the mold and compressed at room temperature to form a dense preform (blank). The process requires careful attention to compression ratio and molding shrinkage rate.

  • Compression Ratio: Typically 4-6 for PTFE
  • Molding Shrinkage Rate: Generally 2.6-4.5% for PTFE
  • Raw Material: Suspension polymerization resin preferred, particle size 20-500 microns
  • Preform Pressure: 17-35 MPa
  • Pressure Holding Time: 15 minutes for 100mm thick blanks
  • Important: "Degassing" must be performed during pressing

2. Sintering

The preform is heated above the melting point. Critical parameters must be carefully controlled:

  • Heating Rate: 20-120°C/hour (slower for larger products)
  • Suspension Resin Temperature: 370-380°C
  • Dispersion Resin Temperature: 360-370°C
  • Note: Higher sintering temperature increases shrinkage and porosity
  • Sintering Time: Must be properly controlled

3. Cooling

Temperature reduction from sintering temperature to room temperature:

  • Standard Cooling: Slow cooling at 15-25°C/hour
  • Rapid Cooling: Only for sheets <5mm thick or thin-walled extruded tubes
  • Annealing: Sometimes performed at 100-120°C for 4-6 hours

Fluoroplastic Rolling Process

PTFE films are produced through rolling. The rolling process transforms PTFE films from opaque white to semi-transparent crystal color. Two methods are employed:

Unidirectional Rolling

Reheated films (transparent state) are rapidly passed through two identical rotating rollers of a calender:

  • Rolling Ratio: 1.5-2.5
  • Roller Speed: 20 rpm

Multi-directional Rolling

Sintered and quenched films are calendered in multiple directions to gradually reduce film thickness:

  • Rolling Ratio: 2-2.5
  • Roller Temperature: 150-200°C
  • Steam Pressure: 0.5-0.9 MPa (for steam heating)
  • Note: Multiple rolling passes may be required for optimal results

Fluoroplastic Injection Molding

PFA (copolymer of tetrafluoroethylene and perfluoroalkyl vinyl ether), also known as meltable PTFE, can be injection molded with a wide processing temperature range:

PFA Processing Parameters

Maximum Temperature: 425°C
Decomposition Temperature: Above 450°C
Processing Range: 330-410°C
Moisture Absorption: 0.03% (no drying required)
Insert Preheating: 140°C

Injection Parameters

  • Barrel Temperature (3 zones): 200-210°C, 300-310°C, 350-410°C
  • Nozzle Temperature: Slightly lower than maximum barrel temperature
  • Mold Temperature: 140-230°C
  • Injection Pressure: 40-90 MPa
  • Injection Speed: Slightly slower
  • Holding Time: Not too long
  • Cooling Time: 40-150 seconds

Secondary Processing Technologies

Due to fluoroplastic processing characteristics, some products require secondary processing to achieve usable finished products. Secondary processing techniques include:

Cutting

Similar to metal cutting methods using lathes, drilling machines, and planers:

  • Important: Blanks must be left for 24 hours before cutting
  • Equipment: Lathe, drill press, planer
  • Precision machining capabilities available

Welding

Two methods: hot pressure welding and hot air welding rod welding

  • Hot Pressure Welding: Heated to above 327°C in special clamps with pressure
  • Hot Air Welding: Uses PFA welding rod with heating and pressure to join two pieces

Lining

PTFE and F46 (perfluoroethylene propylene) plastic lining for ferrous metal pipes and fittings:

  • Application: Chemical corrosion and erosion resistant materials
  • Suitable for various pipe configurations

Expansion

Used for manufacturing corrugated tubes, heat-shrink tubes, and heat-shrink films:

  • Continuous expansion process
  • Intermittent expansion process
  • F46 Heat Shrink Tube Parameters:
    • Stretching ratio: 3-7
    • Molten cone length: 10-20mm
    • Expansion mold temperature: 80-160°C
    • Expansion pressure: 0.1-0.2 MPa
    • Traction line speed: 80-500 mm/min

Special Applications and Formulations

PTFE Machine Tool Guide Soft Tape

  • Material: Suspension method PTFE fine particles, crushed and sieved through 20 mesh
  • Filler: Bronze or aluminum powder (200 mesh)
  • Pressure holding: 5 minutes for 100mm high blanks, degas 3 times
  • Sintering: Heating rate 50-60°C/hour, hold at 320°C for 1 hour
  • Cooling: Remove from furnace at 150°C
  • Pre-cutting treatment: Preheat blank to 80°C, hold for 1 hour
  • Surface treatment: Sodium naphthalene treatment (naphthalene 51g, tetrahydrofuran 100ml)

PTFE Composite Materials

PTFE can be blended with various polymers and fillers:

  • Polymers: Polybenzene, polyimide, poly(p-hydroxybenzoic acid)
  • Fillers: Graphite, molybdenum disulfide, bronze powder
  • Applications: Hydraulic gates, corrosion-resistant ball valves
  • Molding Temperature: 320-350°C
  • Molding Pressure: 3-30 MPa
  • Setting Temperature: 120-150°C

PVDF Sealing Rings

  • Add 1 part acid absorber
  • Ball mill mixing for 24 hours
  • Mold temperature: 195-250°C
  • Gradually pressurize to 2.5-3.5 MPa
  • Degas 1-2 times during process

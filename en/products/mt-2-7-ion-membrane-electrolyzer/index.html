Three membrane-facing design details

These details are intended to reduce avoidable contact points at the membrane-facing surfaces. Final geometry, material and suitability remain controlled by the approved drawing and order.

01 / ANODE

Flatter membrane-facing mesh surface

The anode mesh orientation is reviewed so the flatter face is presented toward the membrane, helping limit localized contact.

02 / CATHODE

Reviewed spring-element geometry

The cathode spring element is formed and inspected to avoid unintended burr contact before the face mesh is assembled.

03 / FACE MESH

Flattened crossing points

The cathode face mesh is processed to reduce raised intersections that could create concentrated membrane contact.

Questions before quotation

What information is required for an MT-2.7 quotation?

Provide the cell drawing, required configuration, operating medium and conditions, material requirements, inspection scope, quantity and delivery target.

Are the listed materials fixed for every order?

No. The final material grade, thickness, component scope and coating system are confirmed in the approved drawing, quotation or signed order.

Can an existing cell be evaluated for repair instead?

Yes. Send photos, drawings and service history so the engineering team can compare new-cell manufacturing with repair, recoating or membrane-gap retrofit.

Do third-party platform names imply OEM authorization?

No. They are used only to describe compatibility and maintenance requirements unless a separate written authorization is available.

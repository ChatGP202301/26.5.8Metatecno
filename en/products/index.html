Focused on electrolyzer cells, electrolyzer seals and rubber-plastic composite parts for chlor-alkali plants.

Metatecno supplies replacement gaskets and retrofit support applicable to Bluestar cells, Asahi Kasei-type cells, Chlorine Engineers-type cells, DD350 cells, UHDE-type cells and INEOS-type cells.

Our independently developed solutions help chlor-alkali producers repair existing electrolysis units and convert them into high-current-density membrane-gap ion-membrane electrolyzers.

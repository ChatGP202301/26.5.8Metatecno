Product information

Short Introduction

DD350 Anode gasket 2 is a custom sealing product for chlor-alkali ion membrane electrolyzer maintenance and cell repair.

It is used to seal the relevant electrolyzer frame, anode/cathode position or auxiliary connection and reduce leakage risk under caustic, brine, chlorine-side or corrosive chemical service.

Metatecno manufactures the part according to drawing, sample, size and operating conditions rather than by model number alone.

Use cases

Applications

  • Chlor-alkali electrolyzer maintenance
  • Caustic soda production system
  • Ion membrane electrolyzer sealing
  • Electrolyzer cell repair
  • Chemical corrosion-resistant sealing

FAQ

FAQ

Can you quote by model number only?

No. A model number helps identify the possible platform, but quotation also requires drawing, sample photos, size, material requirement, medium, temperature, pressure and operating conditions.

Can you manufacture according to drawing or sample?

Yes. Metatecno can manufacture DD350 Anode gasket 2 according to drawing or sample. Used samples should be checked against critical dimensions because rubber and plastic parts may deform after service.

What information is required before quotation?

Please send drawing, sample photos, material requirement, size, quantity, medium, temperature, pressure, operating conditions and electrolyzer platform or model.

How do you choose the gasket material?

Material selection depends on medium, temperature, pressure, sealing position and operating conditions. EPDM, FKM, PTFE, PFA, FEP, PVDF, Rubber-plastic composite materials can be reviewed according to the application.

Is this an original OEM part or a compatible replacement?

This is a compatible replacement product manufactured according to drawing, sample and confirmed operating conditions. It is not described as an original OEM part.

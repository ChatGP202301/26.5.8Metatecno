Technical assurance

What can be confirmed before an electrolyzer seal quotation?

Metatecno can review a proposed sealing product against the supplied drawing, sample, sealing position and operating conditions. Final dimensions, tolerances, material and inspection scope belong in the reviewed quotation, drawing or signed order—not in a general website promise.

Quotation basis

Information required for a useful technical review

A model or brand name can identify a possible equipment family, but it is not a complete manufacturing specification. A review normally needs the following order-specific information.

  • Drawing, measured sketch or sample photos
  • Sealing position and equipment platform or model
  • Critical dimensions, cross-section, hole layout and tolerance notes
  • Existing material or required material
  • Medium, temperature, pressure and operating conditions
  • Quantity, trial requirement and delivery expectation
  • Failure description when replacing a damaged or short-life seal

Limits

What this website does not prove

  • It does not prove that one material is suitable for every electrolyte, temperature, pressure or sealing position.
  • It does not replace an approved drawing, material specification or inspection plan.
  • It does not establish a service-life guarantee or numerical performance result.
  • It does not establish OEM affiliation, endorsement or authorization.
  • It does not establish the validity or scope of a certificate unless the current certificate is published with holder, scope and dates.

Compatibility and trademark notice

Model and brand names identify compatibility requirements only. Metatecno is an independent manufacturer and does not claim affiliation with or authorization from the original equipment manufacturer unless expressly documented.

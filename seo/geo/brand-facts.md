## Facts held from publication

| Claim | Status | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Founding date or corporate history | D — not approved | Company registration or approved corporate record |
| Named customers or project results | D — not approved | Customer permission and engineering record |
| Certificates and certificate scope | D — not approved | Current certificate, holder, scope and validity dates |
| Numerical performance or service-life claims | D — not approved | Test method, conditions, sample and dated result |
| OEM authorization or affiliation | E — prohibited unless expressly documented | Signed authorization reviewed by legal counsel |

## Review discipline

- Engineering confirms technical meaning, limitations and test conditions.
- Sales confirms the quotation inputs and whether a claim matches the offered scope.
- Legal reviews company identity, privacy, terms, trademarks and customer permissions.
- Native reviewers approve translated meaning after the English source is approved.
- A page with unresolved D or E facts remains `noindex,follow` and is excluded from Sitemap and hreflang.

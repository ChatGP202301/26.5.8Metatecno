# Legal and native-language approval required

The implementation adds a structured privacy notice for English, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Russian and frozen Italian pages. It identifies the inquiry purpose, pre-contract/legitimate-interest/consent grounds, Cloudflare and Google processors, international transfers, 90-day operational metadata, default 24-month inquiry-email retention, and user rights.

Before production approval, counsel must verify the exact controller legal name and address, applicable legal grounds and transfer mechanisms, complaint authority wording, retention periods, and local requirements. Native reviewers must approve Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, Russian and Arabic. Arabic remains `noindex,follow` and outside hreflang/Sitemap until both reviews pass.

Product Overview

Our electrolyzer sealing products are specifically designed for ion membrane electrolysis systems used in the chlor-alkali industry. With over 30 years of research and development, we have perfected sealing solutions that are fully compatible with major electrolyzer manufacturers including Asahi Kasei and Bluestar.

These high-performance sealing products offer exceptional resistance to harsh chemical environments and maintain their integrity across a wide temperature range, ensuring reliable operation and minimal downtime in your production facilities.

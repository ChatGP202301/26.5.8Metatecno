About Metatecno

Metatecno(Chongqing)Technology Co.,LTD is located at No. 302, Zhenxing Road, Chuannan Lingang Area, China (Sichuan) Pilot Free Trade Zone, specializing in the production of sealing products for ion membrane electrolyzers. With decades of research and development experience, our team has developed sealing products compatible with virtually all ion membrane electrolyzer systems, including Asahi Kasei and Bluestar specifications.

Our large-scale composite sealing products offer excellent temperature resistance and chemical corrosion resistance, meeting the demanding requirements of the chlor-alkali industry.
